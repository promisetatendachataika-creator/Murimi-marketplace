Terms of Use

Last updated 15 October 2026

These are the rules for using Murimi Market. We have kept them short and in plain English, because rules nobody reads protect nobody.

1. What Murimi Market is

Murimi Market introduces farmers who have produce to buyers who want it. That is all it does.

We are not the seller and not the buyer. When you agree a deal here, the agreement is between you and the other person. We are not part of it.

2. Money

Murimi Market never holds your money. No payment passes through this site. Once a farmer accepts an offer, the two of you arrange payment and delivery yourselves, in whatever way you both agree.

This means we cannot refund you, release a payment, or recover money for you, because we never had it. Please take the same care you would at any market.

3. Meeting and paying safely

We share the other person's WhatsApp number only once an offer is accepted. After that:

  • Meet in a public place where other people are around, or at a known farm or business.
  • See the produce before you pay for it.
  • Be careful about paying in full in advance for produce you have not seen.
  • Tell someone you trust where you are going.

4. "Murimi Verified"

A Murimi Verified badge means one thing: a Murimi field officer visited that farm and checked the farmer's identity document and their land, on the date shown.

It does not mean we have inspected the produce in any particular listing, or that we guarantee the farmer will deliver. Check the produce yourself.

5. Your account

  • Use your own WhatsApp number, not someone else's.
  • One account per person. Do not create extra accounts to send yourself offers or ratings.
  • Keep your password to yourself. Anything done from your account is treated as done by you.
  • You must be 18 or older.
  • Tell us if you think someone else is using your account.

6. Listing produce

  • List only produce you actually have, or will genuinely have by the date you give.
  • Photos must be of your own produce, not pictures taken from the internet.
  • The price you list is the price you are willing to sell at.
  • Keep the quantity honest, and mark a listing sold out when it is.

7. Offers and orders

An offer is a genuine intention to buy. When a farmer accepts it, both of you should expect the deal to happen at the price and quantity agreed.

Changing a listing's price later does not change offers already sent. They stay as they were sent, and the farmer can accept or decline them.

8. Ratings

Rate honestly, and only after a real deal. Do not rate yourself through another account, and do not offer anyone anything in exchange for a good rating.

9. Things you must not do

  • Post false listings, false prices, or produce you do not have.
  • Harass, threaten or abuse anyone, on the site or on WhatsApp afterwards.
  • Collect people's phone numbers from the site for advertising or to sell on.
  • Try to break into the site, other people's accounts, or our systems.
  • Use the site for anything illegal.

10. What we can do

We may hide a listing, or block an account, when we believe these rules have been broken, or to protect other people. Where we reasonably can, we will tell you why.

Anyone can report a listing to us using the "Report this listing" link.

11. What we do not promise

We try to keep Murimi Market working and accurate, but we cannot promise that it will always be available, or that every listing, price or person on it is genuine.

Market prices shown on the site are a guide gathered from markets such as Mbare Musika. They are not a valuation and not a promise of what you will get.

Because the deal is between you and the other person, we are not responsible for what they do: produce that is not as described, a buyer who does not pay, or a delivery that does not arrive. Nothing in these terms takes away any right you have under Zimbabwean law.

12. When something goes wrong

Talk to the other person first. Most problems are misunderstandings about timing or quantity.

If that does not work, WhatsApp us on +263 78 257 3320. We can look at what the site recorded, and we can act on accounts that break these rules. We cannot force anyone to pay or deliver.

13. Changes

We may update these terms as the service grows. The date at the top shows when they last changed. If a change matters, we will say so on the site.

14. Law

These terms are governed by the laws of Zimbabwe.
