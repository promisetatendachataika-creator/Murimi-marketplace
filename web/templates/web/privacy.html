Privacy Notice

Last updated 15 October 2026

Your phone number is how people reach you, so we are careful with it. This page says exactly what we keep, who can see it, and how to get it back or removed.

The short version

  • Your phone number is not public. Nobody sees it until you accept an offer, or they accept yours.
  • Your full name is not public either. Buyers see "Tendai M.", not your full name. Shops and schools are shown by their business name, because that is the point of a business account.
  • We remove the location from your photos before anyone sees them.
  • We cannot read your password. Nobody can, including us.
  • We do not sell your details to anybody.

Who can see what

Anyone visiting the site can see: your produce, price, quantity, province and district, your photos, your short name ("Tendai M.") or business name, whether you are Murimi Verified, and your star rating and number of completed orders.

Only the person you are dealing with can see: your full name and your WhatsApp number, and only once an offer has been accepted. Before that, neither side has the other's number.

Nobody except you can see: your password, your email address, and your login history.

Our staff can see your account details and your orders, so they can help when something goes wrong and deal with reports. Staff cannot read your password, and cannot change an agreed price or quantity on an order.

Photos

Phone cameras often record the exact GPS location where a photo was taken. When you upload a photo, our server saves it again as a new image, and that removes the location and other hidden camera information. The photo buyers see does not carry your farm's coordinates.

Passwords

We never store your actual password. We store a scrambled fingerprint of it that cannot be turned back into the password. When you log in we scramble what you typed and compare the two. This is why we can reset your password but can never tell you what it was.

WhatsApp confirmation codes

When we send you a 6-digit code, we store only a fingerprint of it, not the code itself. It stops working after 10 minutes, or after 5 wrong tries.

To deliver those codes to WhatsApp we must pass your number and the message to Meta, who own WhatsApp. They handle it under their own terms. While the site is still being tested, no messages are sent at all.

Who else we share with

We do not sell your details, and we do not give them to advertisers.

We share only what is necessary with:

  • The person you are trading with, as described above.
  • The company that hosts the website, which stores the data for us.
  • Meta, to deliver WhatsApp codes, once that is switched on.
  • The authorities, if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Your account details stay while your account is open. Listings, orders and ratings are kept as the record of what happened, because both sides may need to refer back to a deal.

If you close your account, we remove your name, phone number and email. We keep the record that an order happened, without your personal details attached, so the other person's own history still makes sense.

What you can ask for

You can ask us to:

  • Tell you what we hold about you
  • Correct anything wrong
  • Delete your account
  • Explain anything on this page

WhatsApp us on +263 78 257 3320 and we will deal with it. You can also change your name, username and email yourself in My account.

Keeping it safe

Passwords are stored scrambled. Wrong-password attempts are limited, so nobody can guess their way in. Photos are checked before they are accepted. We check our own work with automated tests before every change goes live.

No website can promise it will never be broken into. If something does happen to your information, we will tell you and the authorities, as the law requires.

Children

Murimi Market is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly keep information about children. If you believe we have, tell us and we will remove it.

Changes

If we change what we collect or who sees it, we will update this page and change the date at the top.
