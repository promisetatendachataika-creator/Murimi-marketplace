How it works

  1. List your harvest

    Crop, quantity, price and a photo. We show you today's market price while you type, so you can price it fairly.

  2. Get offers

    Buyers send offers. You accept or decline. Your phone number is only shared after you accept.

  3. Deliver and get rated

    Mark the order done. Good ratings and a Murimi Verified badge bring you repeat buyers.
